# Audio Sources

Provenance registry for every audio file under `public/assets/`. Required by CLAUDE.md
"Build Guards & Asset Policy": no audio file may be added without a row here, and only
**CC0** or **self-generated** audio is allowed — an unresolved copyright claim on a sound
is one of the most common rejection reasons on Playables.

Lives at the repo root, not under `public/assets/`, deliberately: it's an internal
process document, not a game asset — it must never ship inside `dist/` or the submission
ZIP (see CLAUDE.md "Build Guards & Asset Policy").

**Most of this game's sound is not in this table at all**, and that is the design: `src/audio/sfx.ts`
renders six of its eight effects from arithmetic at boot, so they are self-generated by construction
and there is nothing to license. The two rows below are the deliberate exception — a coin and an
impact are *objects striking things*, which a waveform cannot be. The packs they came from are kept
under `dev-assets/audio/` (not shipped) along with `measure.py`, the instrument the picks were made
with.

## The music, and the one thing this registry cannot establish for it

`audio/music.mp3` is a Stable Audio render supplied by the project owner. That is what makes it
**self-generated** rather than stock, and it is why the file is in the build at all — it was pulled
once, and the reason it was pulled is worth keeping.

### ⚠ What went wrong the first time

An earlier track (`upbeat-road-trip-surf-rock_090126.mp3`, 2.76MB) sat in this registry with **no
source URL and no licence**, i.e. the one entry that satisfied neither half of the rule at the top
of this document. It was also, on its own, the reason `npm run build` was red: `dist/` came to
8.07MB against this project's own 8MB working ceiling. Both problems had one fix at the time — the
file went out and `src/audio/music.ts` rendered a chiptune bed instead.

**What changed is the fact, not the rule.** The provenance was supplied: these tracks are generated,
not downloaded. A generated track is self-generated by the same argument the rendered tones are, so
the rule is met rather than bent, and a recording is worth 1.85MB of ceiling in a way an
unattributable one never was.

### ⚠ The residual, stated rather than assumed

**This document cannot verify a generative model's commercial terms, and nobody but the account
holder can.** What has to be true before submission, and is not established here:

- the Stable Audio plan the render was made on **grants commercial use** of its output (Stability's
  free tier historically did not; a paid plan does) — a render made on a non-commercial tier is the
  same problem as an unlicensed stock track wearing a different label;
- and it must need **no attribution shown in-game**, because this game has nowhere to put a credit
  line. A "free with attribution" grant cannot ship here without building a credits surface first.

If either is not true, the answer is not a different file: it is `src/audio/music.ts`, which is
still in the build and still what plays if this file is absent (see `Preloader.create`). What that
costs is stated plainly — a chiptune bed is not a recording and is not pretending to be one.
